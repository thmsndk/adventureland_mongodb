Community host — this is an independent Adventure Land fork operated separately from the official game at adventure.land.

Progress, characters, banks, and leagues here are not shared with the official Standard realm. Community content (for example the bee dungeon) exists only on this host.

Real-money shell purchases are disabled on Community. In-game shells may still drop or be earned; they cannot be bought with Stripe here.

Game code is used under the Adventure Land open-source license (AdventureLandOnlyUse). This site includes attribution to https://adventure.land as required.
